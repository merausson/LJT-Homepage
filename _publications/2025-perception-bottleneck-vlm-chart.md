This paper investigates the perception bottleneck of Vision-Language Models (VLMs) for chart understanding. I am the first author, and the co-authors are Weihao Zeng, Xiwen Zhang, Yijun Wang, Zifei Shan, and Junxian He. The code (Vision4Chart) is available on GitHub.
