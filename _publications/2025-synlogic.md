This paper studies synthesizing verifiable reasoning data at scale for learning logical reasoning and beyond. I am the first author, and the co-authors are Yuanxiang Fan, Zhuo Jiang, Han Ding, Yongyi Hu, Chi Zhang, Yiqi Shi, Shitong Weng, Aili Chen, Shiqi Chen, Yunan Huang, Mozhi Zhang, Pengyu Zhao, Junjie Yan, and Junxian He. The code is available on GitHub.
